Imtihan Hub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Imtihan Hub ("the app") is a study app for Pakistani students: MCQ practice, mock tests, merit calculators and links to official board, university and testing-body websites.

Information we collect

The app has no account and no sign-up. Your name (optional), class, board, goal, practice history, bookmarks, exam countdowns and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us.

Leaderboard (optional)

Only if you choose Join the leaderboard, the app creates an anonymous Google Firebase account (no email or phone number) and stores the display name you type together with your weekly points, number of quizzes and weekly grand-test score, time taken and submission time. Other app users can see this name and these scores. You can leave at any time in Settings → Leaderboard; scores can be deleted on request by email. Firebase is operated by Google (Firebase privacy).

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily study reminder or add an exam countdown, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. No data leaves your device for this.

Network requests

The app downloads public files: a notice list (results, admissions) and study content (new MCQs, past papers, Urdu translations, test-pattern updates). These requests send no personal data. Official links open the websites of boards, universities and testing bodies, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is meant for students. It does not ask for or collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Your study data is on your device. Use More → Settings → Clear my progress, or uninstall the app, to delete it. To delete leaderboard entries, turn the leaderboard off and email us your display name.

Disclaimer

Imtihan Hub is independent and is not affiliated with any board, commission, university or government body. Always confirm dates, patterns and results on the official website.
